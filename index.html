🚀 6 Core Power Functions & Real-World Benefits

1 · VISUAL LIGHT-TABLE

📁 Multi-PDF Merge, Reorder, Split & Page Stamping

Drop multiple PDFs from your computer, phone, or Google Drive and see every page laid out like a physical light table.

  • Drag & Drop Across Files: Interleave pages from different PDFs in any order.
  • Visual "DELETE" Stamp: Mark unwanted pages before deleting or merging, with instant Undo.
  • One-Click Split & Share: Extract selected pages into a separate PDF or share directly via WhatsApp/Email.
2 · STUDIO CAMERA SCANNER

📷 Smart Document Scanner with 8-Point Crop & Deblur

Turn mobile camera photos or WhatsApp document images into clean, professional multi-page A4 PDFs.

  • 8-Handle Interactive Crop: Drag any corner or side edge to trim desk backgrounds accurately.
  • 4 Studio Color Modes: Original Color, Magic Color (Vivid Text), B&W Document, and Grayscale.
  • Clarity & Deblur Engine: Unsharp-mask sharpening + Brightness/Contrast controls make blurry text crisp.
3 · EDIT SCANNED DOCUMENTS

🔎 In-Place Find & Replace on Scanned & Digital PDFs

Need to correct a name, date, amount, or typo inside a flat scanned image or camera document? No need to re-type the whole page.

  • Works on Camera Scans: Uses OCR word bounding boxes to locate words inside flat images.
  • Smart Background & Ink Sampling: Automatically samples the paper shade and ink color so the replacement blends naturally.
  • Click-Anything Box Editor: Also lets you click any spot on a page to place a custom text patch.
4 · INDIC & NEWSPAPER OCR

🇮🇳 High-Accuracy Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Hindi & Multi-Column OCR

Specially engineered for Indian regional documents, government forms, and multi-column newspaper clippings.

  • Photo & Border Masking: Automatically masks embedded article photos and divider rules so they never bleed garbage into text lines.
  • True 2-Column Reading Order: Separates full-width headlines, left columns, and right columns so sentences never mix across columns.
  • Crisp Matra Recognition: Preserves delicate Gujarati & Hindi vowel matras (િ, ી, ુ, ૂ, ે, ૈ, ો, ૌ, ં) and conjuncts.
5 · TEXT + IMAGE EXTRACTOR

📝 + 🖼️ Extract Text Only, Images Only, or Both to Word (.docx)

Complete flexibility when extracting content from scanned newspapers, magazines, reports, and ID documents:

  • Auto-Cropped Embedded Photos: Automatically detects and crops portraits, figures, and photos from scanned pages.
  • Use Separately or Together: Download individual photos as .png, copy images (Ctrl+V), download Text-Only Word, Images-Only Word, or Combined Word (Text + Photos).
  • Custom Drag-Crop Tool: Manually drag a box over any signature, stamp, or diagram to extract it as an image.
6 · TABLE-TO-EXCEL + CLOUD

📊 Smart Excel (.xlsx) Table Extractor & Google Drive Sync

Turn financial schedules, invoices, and tabular scans into structured Excel spreadsheets and manage files with Google Drive.

  • Automatic Column Clustering: Detects aligned columns in digital or scanned tables and maps them into real Excel cells.
  • Separate or Combined Sheets: Export one worksheet per PDF page or combine all pages into a single master sheet.
  • Google Drive Round-Trip: Save scanned PDFs to your Google Drive folder and re-open them anytime via Folder Picker or Link.
